# Privacy notice draft — requires publisher details before publication

Personal Asset Passport stores inventory records, photos, documents and reminders in this app's storage on your device. They are not automatically sent to our server. Android automatic backup/device transfer is disabled for the application data. Files rely on the Android sandbox and your device protection. Use a screen lock and keep your device up to date.

If you choose Google sign-in, Google/Firebase processes account and authentication information to sign you in and maintain the session. If you explicitly verify your server session, the configured server receives a Firebase ID token and returns account information. The app does not include analytics, advertising or receipt-scanning services in this version.

Exported backups are encrypted using your password. Anyone with both the backup and password can read the contents. We cannot recover a forgotten backup password. Choosing a cloud drive in the system file picker transfers your exported encrypted backup to that provider at your direction.

Opening documents through another app shares that file with the chosen viewer. Review that app's privacy practices. Signing out stops the Firebase session but does not erase your local device vault. Different Google accounts on the same installation see the same local vault. Deleting an asset hides it and cancels its reminders; its soft-deleted local history and old backups may retain information. Clearing app storage/uninstalling removes app-local data but does not remove previously exported files or your Firebase account.

Before publishing, the operator must add its legal name, jurisdiction, contact, retention periods for any server logs, deletion process, processors/regions and effective date. A verified account-deletion flow is a release prerequisite. This draft is documentation, not a claim of legal compliance.
